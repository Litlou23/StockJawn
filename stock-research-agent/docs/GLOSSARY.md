# STOCKJAWN — Glossary

> Standardized terminology for the project. All AI agents and human contributors
> should use these terms consistently. When a term has a specific meaning in STOCKJAWN
> that differs from its general meaning, the STOCKJAWN definition takes precedence.
>
> This document exists because the same concept is sometimes called different things
> across the codebase. It prevents new contributors from inventing competing terminology.

---

## Core Concepts

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery Source** | How a ticker enters the system. RSS feeds, Finnhub activity, and earnings calendars are discovery sources. They answer "which tickers should we look at?" | Research Signal |
| **Research Signal** | Evidence that accumulates on a ticker from any provider. Congressional buys, insider clusters, and options flow are signals. They answer "why should we care about this ticker?" | Discovery Source, Technical Indicator |
| **Technical Indicator** | A computed value from price/volume data (SMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, ATR). Indicators feed into scoring buckets. They are inputs to the scoring engine, not signals in the research signal sense. | Research Signal |
| **Scoring Bucket** | One of 8 independent dimensions the scoring engine evaluates: trend, momentum, volume, volatility, market context, catalyst, learning, risk penalty. Each bucket has a learnable weight. | Scoring Weight |
| **Scoring Weight** | The multiplier applied to a scoring bucket. Adjusted by the learning engine based on prediction outcomes. Stored in `research_scoring_weights` table. | Scoring Bucket, Scoring Breakdown |
| **Scoring Breakdown** | The per-bucket score details for a single ticker evaluation. Shows how each bucket contributed to the final score. | Scoring Weight |

## Prediction Pipeline

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Prediction** | A specific forecast: ticker + direction + timeframe + confidence. Generated by `PredictionGenerator.cs`. Stored in `prediction_candidates` table. | Pick, Candidate |
| **Prediction Outcome** | The actual result after a prediction's timeframe expires. Stored in `prediction_outcomes` table. | Paper Trade Outcome |
| **Candidate** | A ticker being evaluated for inclusion somewhere. Context-dependent — can be a watchlist candidate, prediction candidate, or paper trade candidate. Always check which type. | Prediction, Pick |
| **Pick** | A paper trade candidate selected by `DynamicPickOrchestrator.cs`. Picks are predictions that have been promoted to simulated trades. | Prediction, Candidate |
| **Actionability Tier** | A classification of how tradeable a prediction is (e.g., strong buy, moderate buy, watch). Derived from the composite score. | Quality Tier, Confidence |
| **Quality Tier** | A classification of paper trade candidate quality. Used by `DynamicPickOrchestrator` to rank candidates. | Actionability Tier |
| **Confidence** | A 0–1 value representing how certain the system is about a prediction. Should be calibrated so that 80% confidence ≈ 80% accuracy. | Strength (research signals use strength, not confidence) |

## Watchlist

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Watchlist Item** | A ticker actively being tracked on the dynamic watchlist. Has a composite score and lifecycle state. Stored in `watchlist_items`. | Watchlist Candidate |
| **Watchlist Candidate** | A ticker being evaluated for promotion to the watchlist. May or may not be promoted based on score and capacity. Stored in `watchlist_candidates`. | Watchlist Item |
| **Watchlist Change Log** | A record of additions, removals, and score changes on the watchlist. Stored in `watchlist_change_log`. | — |

## Paper Trading

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper Trade** | A simulated trade using real market data but no real money. Can be stock or options. | Live Trade (does not exist yet) |
| **Paper Stock Candidate** | A stock selected for paper trading. Stored in `paper_stock_candidates`. | Prediction Candidate |
| **Paper Option Candidate** | An options contract selected for paper trading. Stored in `paper_option_candidates`. | Paper Stock Candidate |
| **Paper Outcome** | The result of a closed paper trade. Stored in `paper_stock_outcomes` or `paper_option_outcomes`. | Prediction Outcome |

## Learning System

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning Engine** | The system that adjusts scoring weights based on prediction outcomes. `LearningEngine.cs`. | Learning Stats |
| **Learning Insight** | An auto-generated observation about system performance (e.g., "momentum signals underperforming in current regime"). Stored in `learning_insights`. | Learning Stats |
| **Learning Stats** | Aggregate statistics about paper trade performance. `stock_learning_stats` and `option_learning_stats` tables. | Learning Insight |
| **Signal Performance** | How well a specific signal type predicts outcomes. Stored in `research_signal_performance`. | Scoring Weight |
| **Outcome Evaluation** | The process of comparing a prediction to its actual result and feeding the difference back to the learning engine. | — |

## Research Engine

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Research Run** | A single execution of the daily research pipeline (morning scan or EOD review). Stored in `research_runs`. | Research Signal |
| **Signal Provider** | A class implementing `IResearchSignalProvider` that generates research signals from external data. Congressional trades are the first provider. | Discovery Source |
| **Signal Type** | The learning key for a research signal (e.g., `congressional_buy`, `insider_cluster`). The learning engine uses this to track per-type performance. | Signal Category |
| **Signal Strength** | A -1 to 1 value indicating the directional conviction of a research signal. | Confidence |
| **Gate** | A filter that a raw data point must pass before it becomes a research signal. Gate 1 for congressional trades: buys only, ≥$15K, ≤90-day lag. | — |

## System Operations

| Term | Definition | Not To Be Confused With |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning Scan** | The first daily research run. Discovers tickers, scores them, generates predictions. | EOD Review |
| **EOD Review** | End-of-day research run. Evaluates predictions, updates outcomes, triggers learning. | Morning Scan |
| **Market Snapshot** | A point-in-time capture of market conditions. Stored in `market_snapshots`. | — |
| **Candidate Generation Audit** | A record of why specific candidates were selected or rejected. Stored in `candidate_generation_audit`. | — |
